# EAIRG

## Download \(works on Windows\)
- Go to [releases](https://github.com/Wilberforce22/E.A.I.R.G/releases)
- Download newest ZIP file \(name should be `EAIRG.[version].zip`\)
- Locate the zip file, and extract its contents
- Navigate to the content's directory, and run `EAIRG.exe`[^1]

## How to Play
### Evade
Avoid hidden enemies in the small arena to survive, you start over when the bar above your player reaches 0 (move with w, a, s, and d).


### Annihilate
Use your laser to destroy waves of robots, but beware that it drains your health (aim the laser with arrow keys).


### Intensify
Meet new and tougher enemies as you progress, ranging from flying and grounded robots to even Jim himself.


### Raise
Purchase modifications between waves of enemies to gain new powers, bought using metal scraps from said enemies.


### Good luck!

## Playtesting Notes
- The newest enemy \(added in [V1.5](https://github.com/Wilberforce22/E.A.I.R.G/releases/tag/V1.5)\) often spawns far outside the map
- Difficulty doesn't scale well in the endgame \(if you reach wave 50, you're likely to reach wave 100\)
    - It also doesn't scale well at the start (gets quite hard quickly)

[^1]: When you click play, a tutorial screen will appear. You will need to click again to make it disappear.
